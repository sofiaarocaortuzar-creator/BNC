Knowledge Architecture

A systematic synthesis of peer-reviewed papers across eight research dimensions, mapping the state of knowledge on marine natural capital valuation, governance, and the open debates that define the field's frontier.

Pipeline flow — from methodological foundation to conceptual debates

The eight dimensions are ordered by their logical sequence in the research process: robust data infrastructure enables ecosystem assessment, which must be interpreted through geographic and knowledge lenses, before valuation methods can generate values that feed financial mechanisms and policy instruments. Conceptual tensions run through all layers and shape the choices made at each stage.
